Questions, answered

Will this hurt my credit?

No. Pre-qualification uses a soft pull only — zero FICO impact. A hard pull only ever happens later, if you choose to finance, and you'll know first.

What does CarNimbus cost me?

Nothing. CarNimbus is free for buyers — we're paid by partner dealers for delivering ready-to-drive customers, not by marking up your car.

How do test drives work?

Pick a car, pick a test drive center near you, pick a time. You show up expected, terms already set — no 4-hour dealership ordeal.

Are you a dealership?

No — we're buyer-first. Independent dealers plug their inventory into CarNimbus; you stay in control the whole way.

What happens to my data?

Your email and pre-qual info stay with us — never sold. See the Privacy Policy for the plain-English version.

When do you launch?

We're rolling out across LA County first. Join the waitlist — you'll get exactly one text the day it's ready.
